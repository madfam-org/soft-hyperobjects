# Nehru-collar suit jacket

A single-breasted suit jacket with a **Nehru collar** — a short, standing band collar that meets
edge to edge at the centre front instead of a notched or peaked lapel. Hip-length, set-in sleeve,
no lapel.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #445 — tailoring, T3).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`shank-button-solid`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The Nehru collar is one of the few globally recognised alternatives to the Western lapel, and
most tailoring systems relegate the mandarin collar to an afterthought. Drafting the front without
a roll line and solving the stand collar with the rigour usually reserved for a peaked lapel gives
it its due.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, straight CF) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2) + `collar` (cut 1, Nehru
stand).

## The seam that solves

The stand collar length is the **measured neckline run** (two fronts + back) so it closes cleanly,
and its height is **clamped under a comfort limit** so no size binds the throat, whatever the
sliders do. The sleeve cap is solved to the armscye ring.

## Construction notes

Interface the stand collar so it holds; canvas the front edge. Fully line the jacket. Set five
shank buttons down the straight centre-front.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`shank-button-solid`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `diameter_mm` is driven by this
jacket's `button_dia`, the same parameter that drives the `button_stand` interface.
