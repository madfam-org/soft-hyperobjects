# Three-piece suit vest

The waistcoat of a three-piece suit: a **plain V-neck** single-breasted vest in the suit cloth,
worn between shirt and jacket, with a lining back and a **buckled cinch strap** to nip the waist.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #444 — tailoring, T3).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`strap-buckle`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The waistcoat turns a two-piece suit into a three-piece and lets one suit read as business,
formal or relaxed. Its back cinch is the small mechanism that makes it fit a changing waist
without alteration.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, V-neck + point) + `back` (cut 2, satin, cinch strap) + `strap` (cut 2, buckle).

## The seam that solves

The V-neck depth is **clamped above the point** so the V can never drop below the top button,
and the front point drop is **clamped under a quarter of the front length** so it never falls
below the hem baseline and inverts the hem edge, whatever the sliders do. Front and back share
identical side and shoulder geometry so the seams match.

## Construction notes

Cut worsted fronts matched to the suit and a satin or lining back. Canvas the front edge; fully
line. Set the cinch strap on the back with the buckle.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`strap-buckle`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `webbing` is driven by this vest's
`strap_width`, the same parameter that drives the `back_cinch` interface.
