# Dashiki

The **dashiki** — the widely-worn West African pullover tunic: a loose rectangular-cut
top with a distinctive **faced V-neck**, grown-on cap sleeves, and an ornate woven or
printed border. Its construction is close to rectangular; the elegance is the bold neck
facing and the border print.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, Lane 4 — heritage). Official visualizer
and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> **Heritage note.** This cartridge drafts the **garment geometry only**. The wax-print or
> brocade cloth and the embroidered V-neck facing that carry the dashiki's identity are
> the maker's to supply and are **not** reproduced here. Offered with respect for the
> living tradition, widely worn across West Africa and the diaspora.

## Pieces

Front + back near-identical panels (cut on fold at centre) with a faced V-neck (deep in
front, shallow in back) and grown-on cap sleeves. The shoulder, sleeve-underarm, and side
seams all balance by construction (verified to delta 0).

## Parameters

`chest_girth` (bound to the `chest_bust_girth` landmark), `dashiki_length`,
`neck_v_depth`, `neck_width`, `sleeve_grown`, `body_ease`, and marked `facing_depth` +
`border_depth` zones for the maker's neck facing and border.
