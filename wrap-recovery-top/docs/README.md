# Post-surgery Wrap Recovery Top

A crossover wrap top for **after breast, shoulder, or cardiac surgery**, when raising an arm
overhead is impossible or forbidden. It opens completely flat and closes on hook-and-loop at
the side.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #375 — adaptive).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`hook-loop-tape`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

After breast, shoulder, or cardiac surgery, patients are told not to raise the arm — which
rules out every pull-on top exactly when they most need to dress without help. A flat-opening
wrap that closes at the side lets a wearer or carer dress the body without lifting the arm, and
adjust the fit over a dressing or drain with hook-and-loop rather than a knot.

## Pieces

`front` (one wrap front, cut 2 mirrored — the two cross over) + `back` (cut 1 on fold at CB) +
`sleeve` (soft low-cap, cut 2 mirrored).

## The seam that solves

A wrap top's two fronts **overlap**, so the overlap must be a measured, deliberate amount, not
whatever falls out of the pattern. Each front is the back half-width **plus `wrap_overlap`**,
and the hook-loop run is that same overlap — closure and pattern agree. The overlap is clamped
under 1.4× the back half so a wide wrap on a narrow body cannot cross the far side seam. The
sleeve cap height is bisected against the sum of the measured armholes, and the back neck width
is solved from the front's measured shoulder with the flatten-the-rise clamp.

## Construction notes

Flatlock the inner front edge so no ridge presses the incision. Use soft-loop hook-and-loop so
it never scratches healing skin. Add the optional internal drain pocket to hold a surgical
drain bulb off the wound.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`hook-loop-tape`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `strip_length` (a sew-face flange
parameter) is driven by this top's `wrap_overlap`, the same parameter that drives the
`wrap_closure` interface — the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
