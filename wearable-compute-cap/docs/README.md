# Wearable-Compute Cap

A cap prepared to carry a single-board computer: a crown band + a peak, overlaid with an electronics pocket sized to the Yantra4D `pi-hat-case` enclosure and a marked cable route to a rear battery loop. Fashion Cabinet owns the cap + the pocket plan; Yantra4D owns the printable case. The pocket and route are marked for the maker; no electronics are drafted here. FC-200 e-textile capsule.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, Lane 5 — e-textile). Official visualizer
and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> **E-textile note.** This cartridge drafts the **garment geometry + a routing/pocket
> plan** only. Conductive routes, electronics pockets, and channels are **marked** for the
> maker; **no electronics are drafted or supplied here**. The printable enclosure/mount is
> the Yantra4D solid the pocket is sized to.
