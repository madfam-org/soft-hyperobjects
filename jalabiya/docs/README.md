# Jalabiya (Gulf thobe)

The thobe (thawb) of the Arabian Gulf — a long, ankle-length robe of white cotton poplin,
straight and flowing, with long set-in sleeves, a short front placket closed by small buttons,
and a slim stand collar.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 10 — heritage). Official visualizer and
configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> What makes a good thobe — the way it hangs plumb and flares clean to the ankle — is a
> construction fact, not a fabric one. The flare has to be godets, or the robe pulls sideways.

## Provenance

The Gulf thobe is everyday and formal men's dress across the **Arabian Peninsula**, with
regional collar and cuff variations. This cartridge drafts a **plain everyday thobe**. It
carries no regional insignia and invents no ornament — the geometry is the garment's own
construction logic.

## Why it earns its rank

**The flare is godets, not a shaped side seam.** A thobe reads as a straight column at the
shoulder that flares to a wide sweep at the hem. If that flare is cut as a curved side seam, it
drags the grain and pulls the drape sideways. So the flare is added as triangular **side gores
(godets)** inserted from the underarm to the hem: the body panels stay on grain and hang plumb
while the hem still sweeps wide. This cartridge cuts the body straight and **solves** the godet
flare from the target hem sweep — and it sizes each gore so its slant edges **equal the side
seam**, so the godet sets into the slit side seam **without easing** (the hem width comes from
the splay, not from stretching a longer edge onto a shorter one).

**The placket is short and the collar is slim.** The thobe is pulled over the head; it opens
only at a short neck placket closed by a few small buttons, under a slim stand collar. The
buttons are the Yantra4D `sew-through-button`, and `button_ligne` drives both the printed button
and the drafted placket spacing.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **front** (cut 1 on fold, short placket), **back** (cut 1 on fold), **godet** (cut 2),
**sleeve** (cut 2, cap solved to the armhole), **collar** (slim stand, cut 1), **placket**
(cut 2).

1. Join front to back at the shoulders. Slit the side seams from underarm to hem and set a
   godet into each (its slant edges match the side seam by construction).
2. Set in the sleeves (cap solved to the armhole ring) and apply the slim stand collar.
3. Build the short front placket and work its buttonholes at the `button_ligne` spacing; sew the
   buttons.
4. Hem the robe and the sleeve ends; flat-fell the straight seams.

## Hardware

The buttons are **Yantra4D solids** (`notion.hardware_ref → sew-through-button`, linked):
`button_ligne → button_ligne`, a 4-hole button, `hole_spacing` from the ligne, `hole_dia =
1.6 mm`.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **chest** girth, **robe** and **sleeve** lengths, **hem sweep** and **neck** girth.
Every slider extreme — including a narrow sweep, where a minimum gore is still cut — renders
watertight, and the godet flare is reported in the metadata.
