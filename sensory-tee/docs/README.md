# Sensory-Friendly Tee

A sensory-friendly tee for wearers sensitive to touch: a relaxed boxy front + back with a printed tagless label zone, seams marked for flat-fell / covered construction (no raised inside seam allowances), and a soft wide neck with no scratchy binding. A soft-goods garment — no hardware. Everything that irritates on a conventional tee is designed out and marked for the maker. FC-200 long-tail (adaptive/sensory).

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — adaptive & inclusive /
maternity). Official visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> An open, made-to-measure tee that designs out tags, ridged seams, and a scratchy neck makes everyday clothing wearable for people with tactile sensitivity — including many autistic and sensory-processing-different wearers — from an open pattern anyone can make in any soft fabric.
