# Roll-up Belt Keeper

The travelling belt organiser: a split-leather panel with a row of **buckle slots** that
belts thread through, rolled from the tail and closed by a stud strap, then hung from a
printed rack by a reinforced tab.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #370 — care & keeping).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`belt-hanger`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

Belts coiled in a drawer crack at the fold and lose their keepers; the shop answer is a
moulded rack of virgin plastic. This rolls them flat on split-leather offcuts or waxed canvas
and hangs from a printed belt rack — fabricated on demand, from scrap that would be binned.

## Pieces

`panel` (the belt roll body, cut 1) + `strap` (the stud closure strap, cut 1) + `hang` (the
reinforced tab that engages a belt-hanger hook, cut 2 doubled).

## The seam that solves

The slots must divide the panel into **equal intervals with the first and last slot clear of
the roll's ends**, or a belt at the edge falls out when the roll is stood upright. The slot
pitch is solved from the measured slot run and the requested belt count, then **recomputed**
so the row lands exactly on both side clearances rather than drifting — the land-exactly
discipline the magnetic-placket shirt uses for its magnet column. The slot length is clamped
under the pitch so slots never overlap at a high belt count.

## Construction notes

Saddle-stitch the doubled hang tabs; they carry the whole loaded roll. Cut the slots with a
chisel, not a scissor snip, so a threaded buckle does not stretch them. Roll from the tail so
the buckles nest inward and the stud strap closes over the roll.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`belt-hanger`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `hook_count` is driven by this
keeper's `belt_count` — the rack always has exactly one hook per belt slot. The rack arrived
on the yantra4d 500-push shelf; the FC-400 index logs it `(co-create)`, and this cartridge
links it live.
