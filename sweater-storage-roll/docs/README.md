# Roll-up Sweater Storage Sleeve

A soft muslin sleeve a folded sweater **rolls up inside**: lay the sweater on the open mat,
fold the two side flaps in, roll from the head end, and cinch the roll with a pair of ties.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #366 — care & keeping).
**Pattern-only** — no hardware bridge. Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

Wool stored in plastic yellows and crushes; a breathable muslin roll is what a museum
textile store uses instead, scaled to a drawer. It lets a knit rest without a crease line
and deters moths with a tucked cedar sachet — no vacuum bag, no mothball fumes. It deepens
the thin `care_keeping` family.

## Pieces

`mat` (the base the sweater lies on, cut 1) + `flap` (side flap folded in before rolling,
cut 2 mirrored) + `tie` (cinch strap, cut 2).

## The seam that solves

The flaps fold onto a plane thickened by the rolled sweater, so each flap's width is the mat
half-width **minus half a centre gap** — the gap keeps the two folded flaps from overlapping
and bulking up the roll's spine. Every flap is cut to the **measured** mat edge it hinges on,
so the fold seams match by construction. The tie length is the finished roll circumference
(`π·roll_diameter`) plus a bow tail, not a guess.

## Construction notes

Narrow-hem every free edge before assembly; the mat is unlined so it stays soft. Tack the
ties to the head edge as short straps. Roll firmly but not tight — the point is air.
