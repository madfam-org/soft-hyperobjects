# Dhoti

The unstitched draped lower garment of South Asia (also veshti, mundu, panche): a single long rectangular length of cloth wrapped and knotted at the waist. It is traditionally unstitched — the garment is the drape, not a sewn seam — so this cartridge drafts the cloth itself at customary proportions, with woven-border and drape-reference lines marked. The regional drape styles and border meaning are the wearer's. A soft-goods length — no hardware. FC-200 long-tail (heritage). Offered with respect for the living tradition.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — heritage — South Asian). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> Documenting the dhoti as an unstitched draped length — cloth size and reference marks, not a sewn pattern — records a garment made entirely by draping, keeping a living tradition legible as a commons object without imposing a Western cut on it or reproducing borders that carry regional identity.
