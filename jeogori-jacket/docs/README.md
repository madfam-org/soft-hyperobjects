# Hanbok jeogori jacket (maedeup)

The short upper jacket of the Korean hanbok — cropped at the ribcage, with a deep curved collar
band (**git**) faced in a removable white strip (**dongjeong**) and wide gently-curved sleeves —
in the variant fastened by **maedeup-danchu** (knotted cloth-button) closure.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 10 — heritage). Official visualizer and
configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> The FC-300 [hanbok-jeogori](../../hanbok-jeogori/docs/README.md) ties with **goreum** ribbons.
> This one closes with a Korean **knot-and-loop** — a distinct garment in the same family, and
> why this rank names the `frog-closure` hardware as a Group-B co-creation.

## Provenance

The jeogori is core Korean traditional dress; its proportions differ by era and gender. This
cartridge drafts a plain everyday women's-style **short** jeogori. The **maedeup** knotwork is a
living Korean craft (매듭); this cartridge draws the **closure seat** and defers the knot pattern
itself to the maker, inventing none.

## Why it earns its rank

**The git is a deep curved collar band, and its length is measured.** The git runs down the
right front, around the back neck, and down the left front, crossing over itself at the centre
front (the jeogori laps **right-over-left**). Its length is the **solved** run of the neckline it
faces, so the curved collar closes flat — not a guess. The **dongjeong** is a narrow white strip
cut to the git's visible edge; it is **removable** and washed separately, a genuine hanbok upkeep
detail.

**The maedeup closure is a knot-and-loop, not a button through a hole.** A maedeup-danchu is a
cloth knot on one lap that passes through a corded loop on the other. This cartridge draws the
loop tab and the knot-button seat and wires them to the Yantra4D `frog-closure` solid as a
**co-creation** (`linked: false`, the FC-300 frog-closure carry-over): `span` = the closure
width, `knots` = the knot count.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **back** (cut 1 on fold), **front** (cut 2, lapping), **sleeve** (cut 2, cap solved to
the armhole), **git** (collar band, cut 1), **dongjeong** (white strip, cut 1), **loop_tab**
(cut 1 per knot).

1. Join fronts to back at the shoulders and side seams; set in the wide sleeves.
2. Face the whole collar run with the git (its length solved to both fronts' collar edges plus
   the back neck), then set the removable dongjeong along the git's visible edge.
3. Attach the maedeup loop tab(s) to one lap and the knot button(s) to the other.
4. Hand-finish so the jacket can be unpicked and washed flat.

## Hardware

The maedeup knot-and-loop closure is a Yantra4D **co-creation** (`notion.hardware_ref →
frog-closure`, `linked: false`): `span → closure_span`, `knots → knot_count`. The maedeup
knotwork itself is the maker's craft and is not drawn here.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **chest** girth, **jacket** length and **sleeve** length. The jeogori is short by
tradition, the git is solved to the collar run, and every slider extreme renders watertight.
