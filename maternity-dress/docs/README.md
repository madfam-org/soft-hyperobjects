# Maternity Dress

An empire-waist maternity dress: a fitted bodice to just under the bust, seamed to a gently gathered A-line skirt that falls over and grows with the bump. The high empire seam + skirt flare give room without a tent shape. A soft-goods garment — no hardware. FC-200 long-tail (maternity).

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — adaptive & inclusive /
maternity). Official visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> An open, made-to-measure maternity dress whose empire seam and flared skirt grow with the bump lets a parent dress the whole pregnancy from one adaptable pattern, keeping fabric and fit in the maker's hands rather than buying a series of short-lived specialty garments.
