# One-handed-dressing Jacket

A softshell jacket a person with the use of **one arm** can put on and close alone. The
dressing aid is drafted in, not just named.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #373 — adaptive).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`magnetic-clasp`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

After a stroke or an amputation, putting on a jacket can be a two-carer task. Threading a weak
arm through a normal armscye and aligning a zip one-handed are the two failure points; this
widens the armscye so the arm drops in, and puts the front on magnetic clasps that need no
alignment — a palm shove closes them.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2 mirrored, magnetic-clasp placket cut on) + `back` (cut 1 on fold at CB, with a
drop tail) + `sleeve` (one-piece dropped-shoulder, cut 2 mirrored).

## The seam that solves

The two armscyes are asymmetric in use (the anchor side is entered blind, the working side
threaded) yet **both must receive the same one-piece sleeve** without ripple. The sleeve cap
is bisected against the **sum of the two measured armscyes** plus a modest ease, so one cap
fits both openings. The clasp pitch is recomputed from whole intervals across the measured
closure run so the column lands exactly. The back neck width is solved from the front's
measured shoulder with the **flatten-the-rise clamp** for extreme proportions.

## Construction notes

Bar-tack the armscye corners — the wide opening takes the dressing strain. Back the placket
with a grosgrain stay so the clasp discs cannot tilt under load. The back drop tail is curved,
not stepped, so it flips cleanly over the shoulders.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`magnetic-clasp`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `disc_dia` (a sew-face flange
parameter) is driven by this jacket's `clasp_dia`, the same parameter that drives the
`clasp_placket` interface — the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
