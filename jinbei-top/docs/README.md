# Jinbei (Top)

The jacket of the traditional Japanese summer set (jinbei), worn at home and at festivals: a light, open, kimono-cut top with wide dropped sleeves that closes with inner and outer side ties. This cartridge drafts the jacket in the straight-seam idiom of Japanese garment construction (back on fold + two overlapping fronts + wide sleeves); the sashiko stitching and aizome dyeing are the maker's. A soft-goods garment — no hardware. Traditionally paired with matching shorts. FC-200 long-tail (heritage). Offered with respect for the living tradition.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — heritage — Japanese). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).
