# Pencil Skirt — FC-100 #32

The mini-skirt straight block carried to knee length (`skirt_length` 580)
with a stronger pencil taper (`taper_mm` 25): the hem half-width pulls in
from the hip half-width, and a CB back vent (`vent_height` 160, 40 mm
underlap, marked as internals plus a vent-top notch on the back piece)
restores the walking ease the taper removes. The CB zipper (200 mm, stop
notch) closes with a hook & eye marked as a drill cross above the top stop.
Front on fold with one 130 mm dart per half; back cut 2 with one 130 mm dart
per panel. The `waistband_style` select swaps the waist finish: `band` emits
the straight cut-1 overlap waistband verified with an eased multi-edge check
against the full waist seam; `facing` emits a clean 55 mm single-layer waist
facing strip with its own exact-ease check. Parameter contract:
`project.json` + the `main.py` docstring.
