# Merino ski base-layer set

A two-piece **merino base-layer set** for skiing: a long-sleeve crew top and full-length leggings
in fine merino jersey, cut at slight negative ease as a warm second skin under a shell,
flatlock-seamed. One cartridge, two garments — a `top` mode and a `bottom` mode.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #456 — active_swim). Pure pattern, no
bridged hardware. Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A base layer is the garment closest to the skin in cold-weather sport: wick sweat, hold warmth,
never chafe over a full day under a pack. Merino does the wicking and warming; the pattern does the
fit — any slack traps cold air, any tight seam becomes a hot spot by afternoon.

## Modes and pieces

- **`top`**: `top_front` (crew, on fold) + `top_back` (on fold) + `top_sleeve` (cut 2).
- **`bottom`**: `leg_front` (cut 2) + `leg_back` (cut 2).

## The seam that solves

**One solver drives both garments** so a set drafted together fits together. The top's neck-point
drop is congruent front-to-back so the shoulders match (the crew neckline dips lower at the centre
front only); the leggings' front and back share **one crotch** so the leg cannot twist. Slight
negative ease throughout, nothing clamped negative.

## Construction notes

Cut a fine merino (170–200 gsm) at slight negative ease. Flatlock every seam. A flat rib neckline
on the top, a soft covered elastic waistband on the leggings.
