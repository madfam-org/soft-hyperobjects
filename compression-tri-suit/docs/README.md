# Triathlon compression suit

A one-piece sleeveless tri-suit in **compression lycra**: swim, bike and run in one garment, cut
at negative ease, split down the centre front and closed with a short invisible zipper at the
chest. Short legs to mid-thigh, racer back.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #449 — active_swim).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`invisible-zipper`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A tri-suit does three sports' jobs at once, and its two failure points are the seams and the zip.
Cutting the whole suit at negative ease and flatlocking every seam solves the chafe; solving the
zip to the drafted opening solves the closure a wet athlete has to work one-handed.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 1, zip) + `back` (cut 1, racer) + `leg` (cut 2, short).

## The seam that solves

The chest zip runs the drafted `zip_length` and is **clamped under 0.7× the torso** so no size
runs it past the front; the front and back legs share **one crotch width** so the leg cannot
twist; every panel is cut at negative ease — every extreme stays watertight.

## Construction notes

Cut chlorine-resistant compression lycra and flatlock every seam. Set a silicone gripper at each
thigh hem so the short legs hold. Insert the invisible zipper at the chest.
