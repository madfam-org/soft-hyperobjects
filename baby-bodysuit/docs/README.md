# Baby Bodysuit (Onesie) — FC-100 #92

The classic infant bodysuit in soft cotton jersey: a drop-shoulder tee whose
**front and back both extend past the waist into a rounded crotch flap**. The
two flaps overlap and close with **snap fasteners** — there is no sewn crotch
seam. The neckline is a **lap-shoulder / envelope neck**: cut wide, with the
front and back shoulders overlapping (marked internal), so the bound opening
stretches over a baby's head for safe pull-over dressing.

Front and back are drafted to the **same** leg and crotch curve, so the flaps
stack cleanly for the snaps and both leg bindings match by construction. The
sleeve cap length is **solved by bisection** to equal the front + back
armholes (the `t-shirt-crew` idiom), and the neck and leg bindings are derived
strips: `opening × binding_ratio + 2×seam_allowance`, cut at twice the
finished width to fold over the raw edge. `sleeve_style` selects
short | long | sleeveless.

## Pieces
- **front** — cut 1 on fold; tee bodice → crotch flap (leg + crotch edges, snap marks).
- **back** — cut 1 on fold; same block; the flaps overlap the front and snap.
- **sleeve** — cut 2; short knit sleeve, cap solved to the armhole (dropped for `sleeveless`).
- **neck_binding** — cut 1; derived envelope-neck binding strip.
- **leg_binding** — cut 2; derived leg-opening binding strip (one per leg).

## Construction order
1. Bind the neck opening with the neck binding (envelope; the shoulders lap).
2. Sew the shoulder seams (lapped — front over back) and set the sleeves (cap ↔ armhole).
3. Close the side/underarm seams.
4. Bind each leg opening with a leg binding.
5. Overlap the front and back crotch flaps and fit ~3 snaps at the marked drill crosses.

## Honest simplifications (teaching-grade)
- The lap-shoulder overlap is expressed as a marked internal line plus an equal-length
  declared shoulder seam; a production onesie would draft the lap as separate
  fold-back extensions. The mechanism (wide neck that opens over the head) is faithful.
- The crotch is an **overlap closure, not a sewn seam** — front and back crotch
  edges are identical and left undeclared on purpose, so nothing is falsely verified.
- **Snap hardware federates to Yantra4D** (snap family) via the BOM note; only the
  snap *positions* are drafted here (drill crosses on the lap line).
- Knit ease is generous-but-snug; the fabric card (`jersey-algodon`) carries the
  stretch/shrinkage that the flat block does not model.
