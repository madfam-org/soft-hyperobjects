# Clip-down nursing bra

A wireless clip-down nursing bra in cotton-jersey: a soft cup that drops for feeding by
unclipping a ring at the top, **hinged** at the band so it re-seats, over a stable support
sling and a wide soft band.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 9 — structured intimates). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> Deepens the FC-300 [nursing bra](../../nursing-bra/docs/README.md) (a drop-cup on a wired
> cradle) into the softer, wire-free everyday version worn around the clock.

## Why it earns its rank

Two of a nursing bra's details are genuinely **medical**, not cosmetic, and this cartridge
draws both honestly:

**The drop is a one-handed clip.** The strap detaches at a **ring** sewn to the top of the
cup: unclip, the cup drops on its lower hinge, feed, re-clip. It must be operable with the
**other arm holding a baby**, which is why it is a `bra-ring-slider` and not a two-hand hook.
`strap_w` drives both the drafted strap's cut width and the ring/slider channel, so the clip
threads by construction.

**No wire on the milk ducts.** A wire pressing the underbust while nursing constantly can
block a duct and cause **mastitis** — a real medical risk. So the cup is wireless, supported
by the wide band and a stable **sling** that stays put when the cup drops. The metadata states
this rather than leaving it implicit.

**The cup drops on a hinge, not a hole.** The cup's lower edge stays attached to the band as a
hinge; only the top unclips, so the dropped cup swings clear and re-seats — instead of a hole
the breast is pushed through.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **cup** (cut 2 pairs, doubled, hinge at the band), **sling** (cut 2 pairs), **band**
(cut 2 pairs, CB hook), **back** (cut 2 pairs), **strap** (cut 2).

1. Seat the cup's hinge and the sling's lower edge on the band's cup span (the sling under the
   cup).
2. Join the back wings to the band's back span; close the centre back with a longer hook
   column so the band adjusts as the ribcage changes.
3. Finish the cup edges with picot; finish the band with soft plush-back elastic.
4. Thread each strap through the clip ring at the cup apex and a slider on the shoulder.

## Hardware

The clip rings and sliders are **Yantra4D solids** (`notion.hardware_ref → bra-ring-slider`,
linked), never modelled here. `strap_w → strap_w`, `wire_d = 2.2 mm`. The back hook column is a
standard `hook-and-eye`.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **underbust** and **bust** girths — and because a nursing body changes week to
week, it is meant to be re-measured and re-cut. Every slider extreme renders watertight.
