# Toggle Duffle Coat

The classic duffle: a straight wool-melton coat closed by the signature **horn-toggle-and-rope
fastenings**, with a deep buttoned hood, patch pockets, and set sleeves.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #380 — outerwear).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`toggle`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The duffle is a coat designed to be **mended**: the toggles are the only moving parts, and a
broken one is replaced without touching the coat. Where a modern parka is scrapped when its zip
dies, a duffle in non-fraying melton with printable horn toggles outlives its owner. It deepens
the thin `outerwear` family with the most repairable coat in it.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2 mirrored, toggle stand + patch pocket) + `back` (cut 1 on fold, yoke marked) +
`sleeve` (one-piece, cut 2 mirrored) + `hood` (deep hood half, cut 2 mirrored).

## The seam that solves

The toggle-and-rope closures sit on a **button stand that extends past centre front, mirror-equal
on both fronts** so the toggles meet their loops when closed. The toggle row divides the closure
run into whole intervals with the top toggle clear of the collar seam and the bottom clear of the
hem — the pitch is **recomputed** so the row lands exactly (the land-exactly discipline). The deep
hood is cut to the measured neckline, the sleeve cap bisected against the measured armholes, and
the back neck width solved from the front's measured shoulder with the flatten clamp.

## Construction notes

Bar-tack the toggle loops — they take the full closing strain. Heavy melton does not fray, so
seams can be raw-bound. The traditional tartan-flannel body lining is expected but not
structural.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`toggle`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `barrel_len` (a cord-face flange parameter) is
driven by this coat's `toggle_len`, the same parameter that drives the `toggle_placket` interface
— the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
