# Climbing harness short

A stretch climbing short with an **integrated soft harness**: a durable Cordura short whose waist
belt and leg loops are structural webbing channels, adjusted on ladder-lock buckles.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #452 — active_swim).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`ladder-lock`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> **Not a certified fall-arrest harness.** This is a *soft support* short for bouldering, via
> ferrata and gym use — not a substitute for an EN 12277 climbing harness.

## Why it earns its rank

A soft harness built into a short removes the bulk that bunches under a pack and the guesswork of
a belt that never sits where the anatomy wants. Drafting the belt and leg-loop webbing as
structural channels solved to a measured waist makes a support garment that fits a real body.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, leg-loop webbing) + `back` (cut 2, raised) + `belt` (cut 1, ladder-lock webbing).

## The seam that solves

The leg-loop webbing rise is **clamped under 0.7× the inseam** so the loop never runs past the leg
hem; the front and back share **one crotch** so the leg cannot twist; the short is cut at negative
ease — every extreme stays watertight.

## Construction notes

Cut abrasion-resistant stretch Cordura. Bar-tack every load junction — the webbing carries the
load, the cloth carries the webbing. Set the ladder-lock adjusters on the belt and leg loops;
stitch a belay loop.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`ladder-lock`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `webbing_w` (a webbing-slots flange
parameter) is driven by this short's `webbing_w`, the same parameter that drives the
`harness_belt` interface — the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
