# Aerial-Silk Unitard

The full-body unitard of the aerial-silk and static-trapeze artist: a single stretch skin from neck to ankle with **no waist seam** to dig in under a hip-key, closing at a concealed centre-back Yantra4D `invisible-zipper` so nothing catches the silk or the rigging. Made to measure to the body girths and lengths. FC-500 lane 9 (costume, dance & performance).

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, lane 9 — costume, dance & performance).
Official visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> Aerial circus is one of the fastest-growing participatory disciplines, and the unitard is safety equipment as much as costume: a seam or a zip pull in the wrong place catches the silk. Drafting it to a real body as one continuous skin, with a concealed printable-part closure, makes safe, fitted aerial wear a repairable commons object.

## Provenance

The aerial unitard is the working costume of the silk, static-trapeze and corde-lisse artist: a full stretch skin with **no seam or hardware that could catch the apparatus** — the concern that separates it from a fashion catsuit. The friction zones and the concealed closure are the craft.

## The one-piece solve

A unitard has no waist seam, so the front and back are each a single panel from neck to ankle whose width tracks the body girth at each level (chest, waist, hip, thigh, calf, ankle), all at the same negative ease. The outseam and inseam use identical level widths, so they match by construction and the skin is unbroken exactly where an aerialist folds over the silk.

## The zip handshake

The centre back closes on the Yantra4D [`invisible-zipper`](https://yantra4d.com); `zip_length` drives the concealed tape **and** the drafted CB opening **and** the `cb_zip` interface.
