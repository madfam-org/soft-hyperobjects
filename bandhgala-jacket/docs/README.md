# Bandhgala formal jacket

The **bandhgala** (jodhpuri): a structured, closed-neck formal jacket of the Indian court, with a
short mandarin band collar, a straight buttoned front and a welt breast pocket — the black-tie
equivalent of the subcontinent.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #447 — tailoring, T3).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`shank-button-solid`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The bandhgala is the formal jacket of a civilisation, and like the Nehru collar it is under-served
by pattern systems built around the Western lapel. Giving it the same parametric rigour treats a
non-Western formal register as a first-class garment rather than a costume.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, mandarin, welt pocket) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2) + `collar`
(cut 1, mandarin band).

## The seam that solves

The mandarin collar length is the **measured neckline run** so it closes cleanly, and its height
is **clamped under a comfort limit** so no size binds the throat, whatever the sliders do. The
front is drafted clean without a roll line; the welt breast pocket sits in the traditional place;
the sleeve cap is solved to the armscye ring.

## Construction notes

Structure the front more firmly than a Nehru jacket — canvas the chest and front edge, interface
the collar. Set the welt breast pocket on the left front. Fully line. Five shank buttons down the
straight centre-front.

## Cross-commons bridge

jacket's `button_dia`, the same parameter that drives the `button_stand` interface.
