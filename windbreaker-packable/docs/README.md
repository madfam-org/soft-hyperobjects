# Packable running windbreaker

A featherweight ripstop running windbreaker that **packs into its own chest pocket**: a full-zip
shell with a drawcord hem and hood, cut with positive ease to layer over a running top.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #455 — active_swim).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`cord-lock`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A packable windbreaker is a runner's insurance against weather that turns mid-run, and its value
depends on two things: it has to seal — cinch at hem and hood — and it has to disappear when not
needed, packing to fist size.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, zip + pack pocket) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2) + `hood` (cut 2,
drawcord).

## The seam that solves

The shell is cut with **positive ease** to layer without flapping; the pack pocket is clamped
under the front so the whole jacket can invert into it; the hood neck is the **measured neckline
run** so the hood sets in; the sleeve cap is solved to the armscye ring — every extreme stays
watertight.

## Construction notes

Cut a 15-denier DWR ripstop so the whole jacket packs small. Run drawcord channels at the hem and
hood with cord-locks. Bind or flat-fell the seams. A lightweight full-length front zip.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`cord-lock`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `cord_dia` is driven by this jacket's
`drawcord_dia`, the same parameter that drives the `hem_cinch` interface.
