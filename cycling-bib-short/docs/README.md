# Cycling bib short

The road cyclist's bib short: a high-compression lycra short held up not by a waistband but by
**bib straps over the shoulders**, so nothing constricts the belly in the aero position and the
chamois stays put.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #450 — active_swim). Pure pattern, no
bridged hardware. Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The bib short made long-distance road cycling comfortable by removing the waistband a compression
short would dig into the abdomen, and by keeping the chamois — the most important contact point
between rider and bike — from shifting.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, chamois) + `back` (cut 2, raised) + `strap` (cut 2, mesh).

## The seam that solves

The bib strap length is **solved from the torso rise plus the shoulder span and floored positive**
so it always reaches, whatever the sliders do; the front and back share **one crotch point** so
the chamois sits true and the leg cannot twist. Strong negative ease throughout.

## Construction notes

Cut high-compression lycra for the panels and breathable power mesh for the straps. Seat a
multi-density chamois in the crotch window. Set a wide silicone gripper at each leg hem. Flatlock
every seam.
