# Magnetic-close Dress Shirt (adaptive)

A proper dress shirt that **closes without a pinch**: decorative shank buttons sit on the
outer placket for looks; underneath, a column of magnetic button covers snaps the shirt shut
with a knuckle nudge.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #371 — adaptive).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`magnetic-button-cover`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

Buttons are the single most-cited barrier to independent dressing for anyone with arthritis,
tremor, or the use of one hand. A shirt that reads as ordinary but closes with a nudge lets a
wearer dress at their own pace and keep the dignity of a normal wardrobe. The magnetic cover
is a printable solid whose snap lip fits a standard shank button, so existing shirts convert
with the same hardware — the accessibility is retrofittable.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2 mirrored, placket cut on) + `back` (cut 1 on fold at CB) + `sleeve` (one-piece
dropped-shoulder, cut 2 mirrored) + `collar` (convertible, cut 2 on fold).

## The seam that solves

A magnetic pair only holds if the two magnet **centres land on top of each other** when the
shirt is closed. The button and buttonhole stands are mirror-equal about centre front, and
the magnet pitch is **recomputed** from whole intervals across the measured closure run so the
column lands exactly on both end clearances instead of drifting. The back neck width is solved
from the front's measured shoulder — with the **flatten-the-rise clamp** when the vertical
drop would exceed the shoulder run, so the drawn back-neck rise always tracks the flattened
horizontal run. The sleeve cap height is bisected against the measured armholes.

## Construction notes

Fuse the placket before setting the covers; an unfused placket lets the magnets tilt and lose
their grip. Sew the visible shank buttons to the outer placket as decoration only — they never
pass through a hole. Keep the bib pocket clear of the magnet column so a phone never parks on
a magnet.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`magnetic-button-cover`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `button_dia` (a sew-ring
flange parameter) is driven by this shirt's `button_diameter`, the same parameter that drives
the `magnetic_placket` interface — the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
