# Falda de Manta

The **falda de manta** — the gathered skirt of traditional Mexican dress: wide widths of
manta (plain cotton) or woven cloth gathered onto a fitted waistband, full and swishing,
finished with embroidered or ribbon bands at the hem. The fullness is the point.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, Lane 4 — heritage). Official visualizer
and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> **Heritage note.** This cartridge drafts the gathered **garment geometry only**. The
> deshilado (drawn-work), embroidery, and ribbon (listón) decoration that carries regional
> identity is the maker's to supply and is **not** reproduced here. Offered with respect.

## Pieces

A skirt rectangle (cut on fold at CB) whose width is the waist × the gather ratio,
gathered onto a fitted double-height waistband; a marked hem band for the maker.

## Parameters

`waist_girth` (bound to the `waist_girth` landmark), `skirt_length`, `gather_ratio`
(fullness), `band_height`, and a marked `hem_band` depth.
