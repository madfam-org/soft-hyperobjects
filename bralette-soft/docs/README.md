# Soft triangle bralette

A wireless soft-cup bralette in cotton-modal: two **true triangles** of doubled jersey on an
elastic underband, held by adjustable ring-and-slider straps.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 9 — structured intimates). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> The honest opposite of the underwire bra. Where that garment *solves* a wire, this one
> places a soft triangle and says plainly that it does not lift.

## Why it earns its rank

The bralette is the bra a great many people actually wear, and the one the industry mystifies
least honestly — sold in vague S/M/L when its whole point is that it fits softly and needs no
cup sizing. This cartridge draws it as what it is:

**A true triangle, plus a fold.** The cup is a real triangle of doubled cloth: lower edge on
the band, inner edge to centre front, apex carrying the strap ring. Drafting it as a triangle
(not a scaled bra cup) is what gives the soft, unmoulded look. The only shaping is an optional
`fullness` of gathers along the band edge, for a little more room.

**The strap is adjustable through a ring and a slider.** The strap runs from the band, up
through a **ring** at the cup apex, over the shoulder, and back through a **slider** — the
same `bra-ring-slider` hardware the nursing and swim cartridges use. `strap_w` drives **both**
the drafted strap's cut width **and** the ring/slider channel, so the webbing threads by
construction.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **cup** (cut 2 pairs, doubled), **underband** (cut 2 pairs, CB hook), **strap** (cut 2).

1. Fold/double each triangle cup and finish its outer and inner edges with picot elastic.
2. Gather the cup's lower edge onto the band's cup span (the `fullness` eases in here).
3. Join the two underband halves, close the centre back with a small hook-and-eye (or make it
   pull-on), and finish the band's lower edge with plush-back elastic — the band's light
   negative ease is the only support a wireless bralette has.
4. Thread each strap through the ring at the cup apex and the slider on the shoulder.

## Hardware

Rings and sliders are **Yantra4D solids** (`notion.hardware_ref → bra-ring-slider`, linked),
never modelled here. `strap_w → strap_w`, `wire_d = 1.6 mm`. The optional back hook is a
standard `hook-and-eye`.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **underbust** and **bust** girths — the cup triangle from the surplus, the band
from the underbust. Every slider extreme (spread, rise, fullness, band ease) renders
watertight.
