# Shaping Bodysuit

A compression bodysuit shaped by princess seams rather than by darts or by stretch alone. Made to measure to bust, waist and hip girths plus body rise and front neck-to-waist. FC-300 lane 3 (structured intimates).

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-300, lane 3 — structured intimates).
Official visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> Shaping garments fail in two predictable places: at dart tips, where tension concentrates until the stitching blows out, and at the size label, which collapses three independent body measurements into one letter.

## Why princess seams, not darts

A dart takes fullness out at a **point** and leaves a bubble either side of it. A
princess seam takes the same fullness out along a **continuous curve** — armhole, over
the bust, in to the waist, out over the hip. In a compression garment that matters
twice over:

- A dart tip under tension is a **stress riser** that eventually blows out.
- A seam is stiffer than the cloth around it, so a princess line is also a
  **structural line** — doing the job boning does in a corset, with no boning at all.

Four princess seams per side, all built from **one shared curve profile** reused verbatim
on both mating panels, so every seam balances to the micron (`646.4 mm` on both sides,
delta `+0.00`).

## Shaping is stated, not left to the fabric

| Ring | Body | Finished |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| Bust | 940.0 mm | 827.2 mm |
| Waist | 760.0 mm | 628.8 mm |
| Hip | 990.0 mm | 871.2 mm |

All-over `compression_pct` (12 %) applies to every ring; the waist takes
`waist_reduction` (40 mm) **on top** of it — 760 × 0.88 − 40 = 628.8.

## The crotch closure is point/slot

The gusset closes on a Yantra4D `hook-and-eye` tape. Hooks engage eyes: there is **no
sewn flange** whose length must match a garment edge, so only the tape's column and row
counts cross the bridge (`columns: 3`, `rows: 2`) and no dimensional edge coupling is
required — contrast the wire and boning handshakes elsewhere in this lane.
