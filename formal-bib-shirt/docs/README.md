# Bib-front formal shirt

The stiff-fronted evening dress shirt: a **marcella piqué or pleated bib** across the chest, worn
under a tailcoat or dinner jacket, with studs down the bib and a wing or turndown collar.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #443 — tailoring, T3).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`sew-through-button`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The bib is a small panel with an outsized power to look wrong: too wide and it shows past the
jacket, too long and it buckles into the trousers, too short and it rides up above the waistcoat.
Solving the bib to the chest makes the shirt fit the real body it is cut for.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, placket) + `bib` (cut 1, stiff panel) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2)
+ `collar` (cut 1, wing/turndown).

## The seam that solves

The bib width is **clamped under 1.6× the front half** so it never exceeds the shirt front (which
would push the bib edge past the side seam), and the bib drop is **clamped under 0.7× the shirt
length** so it never buckles into the trousers — whatever the sliders do. The collar length is
the measured neck run; the sleeve cap is solved to the armscye ring.

## Construction notes

Cut the bib in marcella piqué (white-tie) or pleated poplin (black-tie) and interface it stiff.
Interface the collar so it stands. Apply the bib to the shirt front; set four studs down its
centre.
