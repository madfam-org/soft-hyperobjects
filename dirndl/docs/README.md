# Dirndl

The traditional Alpine (Bavarian/Austrian) dress: a fitted bodice waist-seamed to a full gathered skirt, worn with a separate tied apron. This cartridge drafts the garment geometry — bodice, gathered skirt, and apron — and marks (does not reproduce) the regional trims, lacing, and apron decoration. A soft-goods garment — no hardware. FC-200 long-tail (heritage). Offered with respect for the living tradition.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — heritage — Alpine). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> An open, made-to-measure dirndl pattern keeps a living heritage garment makeable and repairable by the people who wear it — bodice, skirt, and apron drafted to real measurements, with the regional decoration left to the maker rather than dictated or sold back.
