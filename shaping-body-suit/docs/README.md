# Shaping bodysuit (scoop tank)

A scoop-neck tank bodysuit in power-mesh: a front and a back panel that run from the
shoulders to a **hook-and-eye gusset** at the crotch, so it cannot ride up out of a skirt or
trousers.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 9 — structured intimates). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> Where the FC-300 [shaping bodysuit](../../shaping-bodysuit/docs/README.md) is a
> princess-seamed shapewear piece, this is the everyday tank-cut version worn as a top —
> whose whole appeal is a clean line held down by the crotch closure.

## Why it earns its rank

**It stays down because of the gusset, not the fabric.** The reason a bodysuit beats a
tucked-in top is the crotch closure — the part cheap bodysuits skimp or misplace so they gape
or dig. The closure is the Yantra4D **hook-and-eye** (3 columns × 2 rows), and the gusset is
drafted to seat that plate, with the crotch edges solved so front and back meet it squarely.

**The compression is light and even.** This is a bodysuit worn as a top, not shapewear, so
the negative ease is light and uniform — no zoned squeeze. The finished bust/waist/hip rings
are printed so it stays a number, and the neckline, armscyes and leg openings are scooped and
bound flat for a clean edge under a jacket.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **front** (cut 1, straps cut in one), **back** (cut 1, higher neckline), **gusset**
(cut 2 — self + cotton lining).

1. Join front to back at both side seams and both shoulder straps.
2. Bridge the crotch with the gusset (its two ends sew to the front and back crotch flats,
   both `gusset_w` wide) and enclose a cotton lining.
3. Seat the hook-and-eye plate on the gusset's marked footprint so the bodysuit opens to be
   stepped into and hooked shut.
4. Bind the neckline, armscyes and leg openings flat.

## Hardware

The gusset closure is a **Yantra4D solid** (`notion.hardware_ref → hook-and-eye`, linked),
3 columns × 2 rows, never modelled here — the gusset is drafted to seat it. This is what keeps
the bodysuit from riding up.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **bust**, **waist** and **hip** girths plus the **shoulder-to-crotch** length — the
measurement a bodysuit most needs and a size grid most rounds. Every slider extreme (torso
length, neck/arm scoop, gusset width, compression) renders watertight.
