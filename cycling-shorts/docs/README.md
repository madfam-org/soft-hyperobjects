# Cycling Shorts — FC-100 #58 / Short de ciclismo — FC-100 #58

A compression cycling short: essentially **leggings cut to a long mid-thigh**,
drafted in athletic power-stretch with the intimates cluster's **exact elastic
accounting**, an **anatomical crotch gusset**, and a **placed chamois pad**.
Knit, pull-on, deep negative ease — no hardware.

Un short de ciclismo de compresión: en esencia **mallas cortadas a media pierna
larga**, trazado en punto deportivo de alto estiramiento con la **matemática
exacta de elástico** del grupo de ropa interior, un **refuerzo anatómico de
entrepierna** y una **badana colocada**. De punto, sin cierre, con holgura
negativa profunda — sin herrajes.

## Pieces / Piezas

- **front** — Front. Fold-cut on the centre-front line, cut 1 on fold → full
  front. Carries the narrower fork and a long mid-thigh leg.
- **back** — Back (full seat). Fold-cut on the centre-back line, cut 1 on fold →
  full back. Wider fork, raised seat (back rise extra), long leg.
- **gusset** — Gusset + chamois placement, cut 1 on fold (self). A half-trapezoid
  whose **front edge = the front fork half-width** and **back edge = the back
  fork half-width**, so the crotch seams close by construction. A **chamois pad
  placement marking** on the seat locates the purchased padded insert (guide
  only, not a cut line).
- **waistband** — A **wide flat fold-over casing**. Its attach (bottom) edge
  equals the **full measured waist opening**; a wide flat elastic rides inside
  the fold, cut to `waist × ratio` (or bond/raw-cut the edge instead — see below).

## Construction order / Orden de construcción

1. Close the crotch: sew **gusset.front_edge → front.gusset_edge** and
   **gusset.back_edge → back.gusset_edge**.
2. Sew the **inner-leg seam**: front.inseam → back.inseam (the shorter inseam was
   bowed to the longer, so they match exactly).
3. Sew the **side seam**: front.outseam → back.outseam.
4. Place the **chamois pad** over the gusset marking and tack/edge-stitch it in
   (the pad is a purchased multi-density insert).
5. Finish the **leg openings**: silicone gripper elastic zig-zagged into the
   marked leg zone so the hem grips the thigh and does not ride up.
6. Apply the **waistband** around the full waist opening; join the wide flat
   elastic in a ring, quarter-mark, and enclose it in the fold.
7. **Flatlock / coverstitch** every seam to avoid chafe. Ballpoint needle,
   stretch thread throughout. The compression card is heat-sensitive — press
   cool or not at all.

## Honest simplifications (teaching-grade)

- **Chamois pad is a purchased component.** It is located by a placement marking
  on the gusset seat and emitted as a BOM line; it is **not modelled as
  geometry** and federates as a component reference (a Yantra4D pad cartridge).
  Real pads are multi-panel and multi-density. / **La badana es un componente
  comprado**, ubicado por una marca y listado en la BOM; no se modela como
  geometría.
- **Symmetric leg tube.** Front and back share one snug thigh half-width, so the
  side seam (outseam) balances without a separate side-shaping solve. Real
  cycling shorts shape the outer thigh slightly and may add a panel seam. /
  **Tubo de pierna simétrico.**
- **Solved inseam.** Front and back forks differ, so the two straight inseams
  differ; the shorter one is bowed outward by a bisection-solved amount until the
  inner-leg seam closes (delta ≈ 0) — the same technique the leggings cartridge
  uses. / **Entrepierna resuelta.**
- **Waistband as a wide flat fold-over casing.** The attach edge is sewn 1:1 to
  the waist opening (so the declared seam balances); the negative pull lives in
  the flat **elastic cut length** (`waist × ratio`), not in the seam. A raw
  **bonded/laser-cut band edge** (no elastic) is a common compression finish —
  same attach length, drop the elastic BOM line. / **Pretina plana ancha como
  funda doblada.**
- **Negative ease** is applied in the draft (girth × `1 − ease%`), never by
  asking for a smaller measurement. Cut with the greatest stretch (weft) running
  around the body. / **La holgura negativa** se aplica en el trazo.

## Notions / Insumos

No hardware. Elastics, thread, and one purchased chamois pad; every elastic **cut
length is emitted in the BOM in exact mm**, derived from the measured pattern
openings (waist opening × waistband ratio; leg opening × gripper ratio, per leg).
The chamois pad and any reflective trim, if added, are **component references
(Yantra4D)** — purchased, never re-implemented here.

Sin herrajes. Elásticos, hilo y una badana comprada; cada **largo de corte de
elástico se emite en la lista de materiales en mm exactos**, derivado de las
aberturas medidas del patrón. La badana y cualquier ribete reflejante son
**referencias de componente (Yantra4D)** — comprados, nunca reimplementados aquí.

## Fabric / Tela

`poliester-elastano-compresion` (poly/elastane compression interlock, ~15%
negative ease, high recovery, greatest stretch in the weft). Flatlock/coverstitch
seams; heat-sensitive. See `materials/poliester-elastano-compresion`.
