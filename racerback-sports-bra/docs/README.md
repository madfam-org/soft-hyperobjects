# Racerback sports bra

A pull-on **compression** sports bra in power-mesh: a single front panel, a converged
**racerback**, and a firm wide support band. No cups, no wire — the band's negative ease
is the support.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, lane 9 — active/structured intimates).
Official visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> "High impact" is an adjective. The band grip that actually carries the load is a number,
> solved from the ribcage — and this cartridge prints it.

## Why it earns its rank

A sports bra is a **compression** garment — it limits motion by holding the chest close to
the body — as distinct from an **encapsulation** bra that cups each breast separately. This
cartridge drafts the compression kind, and encodes the two things that make it work:

**The support is the band, not the straps.** The load is carried by a wide firm underband
gripped at negative ease against the ribcage. Its finished ring is solved from the
underbust girth and an explicit `band_grip_pct`, and printed — so "high support" is a
number a wearer can reproduce, not a marketing word. The straps only locate the cup; they
do not hold the weight.

**The racerback keeps the straps on.** The back panel narrows to a **racer**: the two
straps converge to a single yoke between the shoulder blades. The yoke half-width is
**solved from the shoulder** so it sits inboard of the shoulder points. This is not styling
— it is what stops the straps sliding off during motion, the failure a straight-strap bra
has in sport.

## Construction notes

Pieces: **front** (cut 1, straps cut in one), **back** (racerback, cut 1), **band** (cut 1,
doubled for firmness).

1. Join front to back at both side seams.
2. Carry the front straps over the shoulders and topstitch them flat to the racerback yoke
   region, matching the racer centre notch.
3. Attach the support band to the assembled front+back lower edge. Finish its lower edge
   with firm plush-back elastic — the band is cut short and stretched on, because it is the
   support.
4. Bind the neckline and armscyes flat.

## Hardware

**None.** The bra is pulled on over the head; the straps are cut in one with the panels, so
there is no adjuster, ring, or wire to bridge. There is no `notion.hardware_ref` on this
cartridge.

## Made to measure

Drafted to **chest** and **underbust** girths plus the strap and band geometry. The band
and chest finished rings are printed in the metadata so the compression is auditable, and
every slider extreme (band depth, grip, racer rise) renders watertight.
