# Grip-pad palm mitt

A **fingerless** palm mitt for a weak or painful grip: a neoprene band that leaves the fingers
free but backs the palm with a compliant ribbed gripper pad, so a rail, a walker handle or a cup
does not slip.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #434 — adaptive / soft-exo).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`soft-gripper-pad`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A weak grip does not need fingers replaced — it needs friction, and fingers kept free for feel.
This adds a compliant ribbed pad exactly where the palm meets the object and nowhere else.
Making the pad the printable part lets its texture be re-chosen for a cane, a walker or a mug.
The assist is in the geometry, not just the label.

## Pieces

`band` (cut 1, the palm wrap with the pad pocket + thumb slot) + `tab` (cut 1, the wrist overlap
that closes on hook-and-loop).

## The seam that solves

The palmar pocket is drafted `pad_length × pad_width` — the same numbers that drive the Yantra4D
`soft-gripper-pad`. The pad footprint is **clamped inside the band interior** and the **thumb
slot depth clamped under half the band height** so it can never split the band, whatever the
sliders do. The tab sews to the band end; the band wraps the palm ring at negative ease.

## Construction notes

Cut the neoprene at a small negative ease so the band grips. Flatlock the pocket edges so the
pad seats flat. Snip the thumb slot on the marked line. Close on the hook-and-loop tab one-handed.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`soft-gripper-pad`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `pad_len` / `pad_w` are driven by
this mitt's `pad_length` / `pad_width`, the same parameters that drive the `pad_pocket`
interface — the printed pad drops into the pocket exactly.
