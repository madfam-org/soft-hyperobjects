# Packable Rain Shell

A featherweight hooded rain shell that **stuffs into its own chest pocket**: a dropped-shoulder
body, a two-panel hood, and a one-piece sleeve, all in ripstop, with drawcord hem and hood face
that cinch on cord-locks against the wind.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #376 — outerwear).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`cord-lock`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A cheap rain jacket is a wardrobe's most disposable item — bought in an airport, worn once,
landfilled because the seams leak. A packable silnylon shell drafted to seam-seal and cinch on
printable cord-locks is a coat someone keeps. It deepens the thin `outerwear` family with the
most-replaced garment in it.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2 mirrored, centre-front zip) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (one-piece
dropped, cut 2 mirrored) + `hood` (one hood half, cut 2 mirrored).

## The seam that solves

The hood must set into the **measured** neckline, not a neck-girth formula, or it strains the
seam in the rain when it is up and loaded. The hood's neck edge is drafted to the summed
measured front + back neck; the hem and hood-face drawcord casings are sized to the cord doubled
plus turn-of-cloth so the cord-lock always fits. The sleeve cap is bisected against the measured
armholes, and the back neck width is solved from the front's measured shoulder with the
flatten-the-rise clamp.

## Construction notes

Seam-tape the shoulder and hood seams or the rain finds them. Use a silicone-coated ripstop so
the shell packs to a fist. Set one cord-lock at the hem and one at the hood face.

`cord_dia` (cord-lock declares no sewn-edge flange, so resolution is by name and cord size).
Two locks: hem and hood face.
