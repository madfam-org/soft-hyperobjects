# Fabric Lint-brush Mitt

A washable mitt of **napped velour** that wipes lint, pet hair, and dust off a coat with a
stroke — the reusable answer to the sticky-tape roller.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-400, rank #369 — care & keeping).
**Pattern-only** — no hardware bridge. Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The sticky lint roller is one of the purest single-use products in the home — a sheet torn
off and binned per pass. A napped velour mitt does the same job, washes clean, and lasts
years, cut from a hand's-worth of towelling offcut. It deepens the thin `care_keeping` family.

## Pieces

`shell` (one mitt face, cut 2 mirrored) + `cuff` (the wrist band, cut 1) + `tab` (the hanging
loop, cut 1).

## The seam that solves

The mitt is a **capsule** — a straight palm run capped by a measured semicircle at the
fingertips. The palm run is floored at 30 mm so a short mitt on a wide hand keeps a real
straight section instead of folding to a zero-area lens. The two shells are the **same outline
mirrored**, so their side and tip seams match by construction, and the cuff length is the
**measured** wrist opening the shells present, not a girth formula. The thumb is a marked
gusset slit, not a separate piece, so a beginner can make it flat.

## Construction notes

Cut with the **nap running toward the fingertips** so the stroke lifts lint rather than
smearing it. Turn the mitt so all seams sit inside and the napped face works outside. Thread
elastic through the cuff so it grips the wrist.
