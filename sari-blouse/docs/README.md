# Sari Blouse (Choli)

The **choli** — the fitted, cropped blouse worn with the sari and lehenga across South
Asia: close-fitting to the bust with a short hem above the midriff, short set-in sleeves,
a back opening, and its signature **bust darts**.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, Lane 4 — heritage). Official visualizer
and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> **Heritage note.** This cartridge drafts the fitted **garment geometry only**. The silk,
> the border, the mirror (shisha) work, and the embroidery that carry its identity are the
> maker's; the bust dart is **marked** (teaching-grade). Offered with respect.

## Pieces

Fitted front (shaped to the bust) + back (shaped to the underbust) on fold with marked
bust/shaping darts + a short set-in sleeve. Shoulder + side seams balance to delta 0.

## Parameters

`bust_girth` / `under_bust` (bound to `chest_bust_girth` / `underbust_girth`),
`blouse_length`, `shoulder_w`, `sleeve_length`, `sleeve_depth`, `bust_dart`, `bust_ease`.
