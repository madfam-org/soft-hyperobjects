# Covert coat

The countryman's town coat: a **fly-front, knee-length** single-breasted covert coat in covert
twill, marked by its signature four rows of stitching at the cuff and hem.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #440 — tailoring, T4, made-to-measure).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`shank-button-solid`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

The covert coat endures because it works in both the field and the city, and it is a specific,
recognisable thing — fawn twill, a fly front, four rows of prestige stitching. Its whole-length
straight body is where a careless flat pattern fails silently: too little hem flare and at some
sizes the hem comes out narrower than the chest, inverting the side seam.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, fly extension) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2, four-row cuff) +
`collar` (cut 1).

## The seam that solves

The hem flare is **floored positive** so the hem can never come out narrower than the chest —
which would invert the side seam into a valid-looking but unwearable outline — whatever the
sliders do. The collar length is the measured neck run; the sleeve cap is solved to the armscye
ring.

## Construction notes

Half-canvas the front; line the body. Face the fly so the buttons are hidden and the CF hangs
clean. Run the signature four rows of stitching at the cuff and hem.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`shank-button-solid`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `diameter_mm` is driven by this
coat's `button_dia`, the same parameter that drives the `fly_placket` interface.
