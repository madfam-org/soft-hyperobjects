# E-Textile Sensor Tee

A relaxed knit tee prepared for wearable sensing: a boxy front + back with grown-on cap sleeves, overlaid with conductive-thread routes (marked traces from a chest sensor patch to a hem-hub bus) and a sensor-mount pocket sized to the Yantra4D `sensor-mount-plate`. Fashion Cabinet owns the garment + routing plan; Yantra4D owns the printable mount. The routes and pocket are marked for the maker to embroider with conductive thread; no electronics are drafted here. FC-200 e-textile capsule.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, Lane 5 — e-textile). Official visualizer
and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> **E-textile note.** This cartridge drafts the **garment geometry + a routing/pocket
> plan** only. Conductive routes, electronics pockets, and channels are **marked** for the
> maker; **no electronics are drafted or supplied here**. The printable enclosure/mount is
> the Yantra4D solid the pocket is sized to.

## The cross-commons bridge

`notion.hardware_ref` → the Yantra4D enclosure/mount, sized so the garment's pocket fits
the printable case. Enforced in CI by `scripts/qa/verify_hardware_links.py`.
