# Thobe / Thawb

The ankle-length tunic-robe of the Arabian Peninsula and the wider Arab world (thobe, thawb, dishdasha, kandura): a long, modest T-cut body with long sleeves, a neck placket, and triangular side gores that flare the hem for an unbroken line. This cartridge drafts the garment geometry in the straight-seam idiom where the side and gore seams balance by construction; the regional neck/cuff treatments and plackets are the maker's. A soft-goods garment — no hardware. FC-200 long-tail (heritage). Offered with respect for the living tradition.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — heritage — Arab). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

> An open, made-to-measure thobe pattern keeps a widely-worn modest heritage garment makeable to real measurements — a clean T-body with gored flare drafted from a handful of straight-seamed pieces — with the regional neck, cuff, and placket details left to the maker rather than dictated.
