# Wrap-front yoga crop

A wrap-front yoga crop / bralette: two front panels **cross over the bust** (cache-cœur) and tie
at the back — light support, a flattering line, no clasp, no wire, no hardware.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #454 — active_swim). Pure pattern, no
bridged hardware. Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A wrap-front crop is the most forgiving support garment to fit: the wearer sets the cross and
tightness by how they wrap and tie — no fixed band size to get wrong, no clasp to press a nerve,
no wire to fail. It is a genuinely inclusive piece of activewear.

## Pieces

`front` (cut 2, cache-cœur) + `back` (cut 1 on fold, scoop) + `tie` (cut 2).

## The seam that solves

The wrap cross is **clamped under 0.95× the front half** so the panels always cross but never fold
back on themselves; the tie length is **solved from the underbust ring and floored** so the ties
always reach round to knot, whatever the sliders do. Front and back share armhole and shoulder
geometry so the seams match.

## Construction notes

Cut a soft four-way stretch jersey at negative ease. Optionally double the front or add a cup
pocket. Run a soft elastic along the underbust. Flatlock every seam for chafe-free wear.
