# Biometric Bra Band

A standalone under-bust sensing band: the compression ring that carries ECG/EMG
electrodes against the ribcage, worn under any bra or on its own. Wave FC3-H
(E-textile II) of the FC-300 commons.

> **This is a garment pattern, not a medical device.** It carries electrodes and
> positions them. It does not measure, monitor, or diagnose anything, and it makes no
> clinical claim.

## What it is

| Piece | Cut | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `shell` | 1 on fold (CB) | The outer compression ring — the layer that holds the electrodes to the skin. |
| `lining` | 1 on fold (CB) | The inner ring, carrying the electrode pocket windows, sew rings, and signal traces. |
| `closure` | 2, mirrored | Centre-front hook-and-eye extension, `closure_rows` of adjustment. |

## Why it earns a commons rank

The commons already has `sports-bra`, `longline-bra`, and `underwire-bra`. This is
deliberately **not** another bra. It is the sensing layer alone — the piece that in
commercial biometric apparel is welded permanently into a garment you then cannot wash,
resize, or repair.

Separating it earns its rank for a practical reason: chest-strap sensing is sold as a
sealed consumable in about three sizes, and it fits badly on most bodies. That is a
**data-quality** problem before it is a comfort problem, because an electrode that slides
against skin gives you motion artefact, not signal. A band cut to a measured ribcage with
a declared compression figure holds its electrodes still. And because the carrier is a
printed part taking standard clinical snap studs, the expensive bit is the commodity and
the bespoke bit is free.

## The seam that had to solve

Two rings, cut for **different stretch**, that still have to sew together flat.

The shell is the compressing layer (`compression`, default 0.12 negative ease). The
lining is deliberately gentler against skin (`lining_ease`, default 0.04, clamped by an
`error`-severity constraint never to exceed the shell's). Cut naively from the same body
measure, the two rings come out different lengths and one ripples inside the other.

The kernel does not recompute the lining from the body measure. It drafts the shell
once, reads its **measured** bottom edge, and scales the lining by the ratio of the two
negative eases:

```
LINING_HALF = SHELL_HALF × (1 − lining_ease) / (1 − compression)
```

The resulting difference is then **declared as seam ease** rather than hidden — the ring
seams are checked as `lining.bottom ↔ shell.bottom` with `ease = LINING_HALF − SHELL_HALF`.
At the defaults that is 334.4 mm shell against 364.8 mm lining with 30.4 mm of declared
ease, and the check passes at every point in the parameter range because the ease is
derived from the same two numbers that produced the difference.

The other quiet solve: the ribcage is a **cone**, not a cylinder. The `top` edge is
drafted `rib_taper` shorter than the `bottom` edge, which is why the band hugs upward
instead of rolling down off the ribs — the single most common failure of a flat-cut
sensing band.

## Hardware bridge

`notion.hardware_ref` → **`yantra4d/snap-electrode-carrier`**.

`electrode_dia` is the dimensional handshake. It is the carrier's `disc_dia` (a `flange`
parameter — the sewn face), it derives the disc thickness, the `sew_holes` count, and the
`hole_dia`, and it is the size of the lining's marked `electrode-window-*` footprint and
the radius of the `carrier-sew-*` drill ring. The manifest's `electrode_seat` interface
declares `electrode_dia`, so the dimensional-handshake lane can verify that the garment's
window and the hardware's sewn face are one dimension.

The carrier is captured between shell and lining, presenting its conductive face through
the lining window to the skin. Standard clinical snap studs click into its bore.

## Construction notes

- **Fabric.** `poliester-elastano-compresion` — a power knit with real recovery. The
  110 °C iron ceiling matters: do not press near the conductive traces.
- **Order.** Mark and sew the carriers to the lining while it is flat. Then join the
  rings. The carriers are inaccessible once the band is closed.
- **Traces.** The `signal-trace-*` internals are marked routes for conductive thread from
  each carrier back to a front-of-band connector. The shell's `trace-channel` keeps the
  runs off the bottom edge where the band rolls against the ribs.
- **Nothing electrical is drafted.** No amplifier, circuit, electrode, or connector is
  generated by this cartridge. The windows, sew rings, and traces are footprints and
  paths for a maker.
- **Washing.** Pop the electrodes out first. That is the point of a snap carrier.

## Provenance

Original draft for Fashion Cabinet. The two-layer compression band with a coned top edge
is standard foundation-garment technique; the contribution here is drafting the electrode
carrier's footprint as a real pattern feature and solving the two-stretch ring seam
explicitly rather than easing it by feel.
