# Kilt (Wrap)

The Scottish Highland wrapped garment: one long length of (traditionally tartan) cloth with a flat front apron, deep knife pleats across the back, wrapped and buckled at the side. This cartridge drafts the garment geometry as a single flat length with pleat lines, apron folds, and strap positions marked; it does not reproduce the tartan sett or clan meaning. A soft-goods garment — buckles are the maker's hardware. FC-200 long-tail (heritage). Offered with respect for the living tradition.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-200, long-tail — heritage — Scottish). Official
visualizer and configurator: [Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabinet.app).

> An open, made-to-measure kilt pattern computes the exact flat length, pleat count, and pleat depth for a wearer's own waist and seat, keeping a living heritage garment makeable at home from a single length of cloth — the sett and clan meaning left to the wearer.
