# Shift Dress — FC-100 #23

The a-line-dress draft straightened to the classic loose shift: minimal hem
flare (`flare_mm`, default 25) over a straight-ish side seam, generous
`shift_ease`, and set-in short sleeves in place of the sleeveless
facings-only finish. Front on fold with internal side-bust darts; back cut 2
with a 20 mm CB allowance, an invisible-zipper stop notch (`zipper_length`
from the neck down), and one internal fisheye waist dart per panel. The
sleeve cap is bisection-solved to the measured front + back armholes (zero
ease, multi-edge seam check, tol 2.0) and the neck facing strip is derived
from the measured opening (`2×front.neck + 2×back.neck + 2sa`, cut 1).
Parameter contract: `project.json` + the `main.py` docstring.
