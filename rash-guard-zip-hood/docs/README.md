# Zip-hood rash guard

A **hooded** zip-front rash guard in swim-lycra: the second-skin UV surf top, split down the
centre front and closed with a full invisible zipper, topped with an integrated stretch hood for
sun and wind on the water.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #453 — active_swim).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`invisible-zipper`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A hood extends UV and wind protection to the head and neck — the parts a surfer burns first over a
long session — but it makes the closure harder, because a wet hooded top is the worst to drag over
the head. That is exactly why the zip matters.

## Pieces

`front_left` + `front_right` (zip edges) + `back` (cut 1 on fold) + `sleeve` (cut 2) + `hood`
(cut 2).

## The seam that solves

The centre-front zip runs the drafted front length plus the hood front and is **clamped under
`back_length + hood_height`** so no size overruns the opening; the hood neck is the **measured
neckline run** so the hood sets in; a shared shoulder run keeps the front-half and back shoulders
congruent — every extreme stays watertight.

## Construction notes

Cut chlorine/salt-resistant swim lycra at negative ease. Stabilise the centre-front zip line
(the tape is not stretchy) while the rest keeps its grip. Flatlock every seam. Set the invisible
zipper up the front and the hood front.
