# Dive-skin swim suit

A full-coverage **dive skin** in thin swim-lycra: a one-piece long-sleeve, long-leg skin worn
under a wetsuit for warmth and easy donning, or alone in warm water against sun, sting and
abrasion. Split down the centre back with a long invisible zipper.

Part of the **Fashion Cabinet Commons** (FC-500, rank #458 — active_swim).
**Yantra4D-bridged** (`invisible-zipper`). Official visualizer and configurator:
[Fashion Cabinet](https://fashioncabi.net).

## Why it earns its rank

A dive skin lets a wetsuit slide on over a body otherwise impossible to squeeze into neoprene, and
protects warm-water swimmers from jellyfish, coral and sun. Both jobs depend on a true second-skin
fit and a back zip exactly as long as it needs to be — no longer.

## Pieces

`front` (torso) + `back` (torso, on fold, CB zip) + `sleeve` (cut 2) + `leg` (cut 2, full-length).

## The seam that solves

The centre-back zip runs the drafted nape-to-seat length and is **clamped under `torso_length +
120`** so no size overruns; the front and back share identical geometry so the side seams match;
the legs share **one crotch** so the leg cannot twist; every panel is cut at negative ease — every
extreme stays watertight.

## Construction notes

Cut a thin chlorine/salt-resistant swim lycra, thin enough to layer under a wetsuit. Stabilise the
centre-back zip line. Flatlock every seam. Insert the invisible zipper down the centre back.

## Cross-commons bridge

Yantra4D **`invisible-zipper`** (`notion.hardware_ref`): its `zip_length` (a tape-edge flange
parameter) is driven by this skin's `zip_length`, the same parameter that drives the `center_back`
interface — the dimensional handshake the hardware lane enforces.
